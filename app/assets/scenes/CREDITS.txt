Ambiance backgrounds: one mountain in four lights (night, dawn, day, sunset), chosen by the sun where the TV is.
Variants of one picture made by the owner with an AI image editor (Gemini), then aligned to the day picture to the
pixel (tools: OpenCV) and brought to 1920 × 1080. Source of the original picture: to be credited here.
The -glass.jpg files are blurred, brightened copies for the clock's glass.

The clock's layout over each photo (glass fill, rim, sheen, shadow) follows couch-tv's (github.com/Seagey/couch-tv, MIT).
